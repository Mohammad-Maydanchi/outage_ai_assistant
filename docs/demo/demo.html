The problem

Outage calls tie up your team.

When a site goes down, someone has to pick up the phone, and stay there.

  • Long holds. Twenty to forty minutes waiting, on a single call.
  • Endless menus. Press one, press two, repeat the menu.
  • Notes by hand. Written up differently every time.
  • Every site. The same call, across many client locations.
“All agents are busy. Please hold.”
Press 1 for support…
How we attack the problem

It takes the outage, makes the call,
and reports back.

  • It receives the outage details for the site: location, address, account number, and PIN.
  • An AI agent calls the provider and works through whatever it reaches, a person, a menu, or a bot.
  • It writes a clean report, the outage, cause, time, and ticket, never making anything up.
